The mossy stand.

Position52.12344°N 8.30538°W
Elevationabout 105 m OD
Facing106°
Recorded11 Apr 2026, 08:54

One hundred and twenty-four metres from Stop 02, and a few metres lower. The difference is not the hill. It is what was planted, and when.

Moss runs along every limb, there is structure at three different heights, and light reaches the ground.

The name of this glen

An account written down at Adair school in the 1930s explains how Glenabo got its name. It names the glen, not this particular spot.
