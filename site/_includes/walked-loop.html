The walked loop

The group walked this loop with a GPS on 29 August 2026, from Stop 1 down the east side of the valley and back up the west side, passing all three recorded stops. It is about 2.3 km round. Where the walk doubled back on itself, the repeat has been taken out.

Download the walked loop as GPX (11 KB). It also carries the three stops and the outlet. It is a record of one walk through unwaymarked woodland, not a surveyed route and not for navigation: read the terms before you go.
