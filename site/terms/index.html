Terms of use

What this site is,
and what it is not.

It is an information site about the Glenabo catchment and Corrin Hill. It is not a route guide, it does not grant anyone permission to be anywhere, and it is not a safety assessment.

Land access

The woodland described on this site is not owned by Cáirde Corrin. Coillte is the landowner and forest manager of Glenabo and Corrin woods, and other sections of the ground described here are in other hands. Nothing on this site is permission to enter, cross, park on, camp on, or carry out any activity on any land. Whether you may be somewhere, and on what terms, is a matter between you and whoever owns it.

The proposals described under New spaces are proposals. None of the work described has been approved or carried out. All of it would be subject to Coillte consent, environmental assessment including screening for Appropriate Assessment where required, and compliance with health and safety standards.

Forestry is working ground. Harvesting, haulage, thinning and windblow change it without notice, and a track that was passable when a photograph was taken may be closed, felled across or under machinery today.

This is not a route guide

The map, the stop positions, the elevations, the walked loop and its downloadable GPX file are a record of where a small number of photographs and recordings were made, and of one walk on 29 August 2026. They are not a waymarked route, they have not been surveyed for public access, and they are not checked for hazards. Seven of the ten stops have not been surveyed at all.

Positions on the map come from the GPS in a phone and are accurate to roughly ten to twenty metres. Elevations come from the group's own GPS track of the trail and an elevation model, and are given to the nearest five metres. The channel network drawn on the map is modelled from elevation data, not walked. Do not navigate by any of it. Going into woodland, near water or onto a hillside is your own decision and your own risk, and you are responsible for your own preparation, equipment, judgement and safety, and for anyone with you.

Accuracy

Everything here is published in good faith and traced to a source, and where a fact is not established the site says so rather than filling the gap. Species determinations are as accepted by the contributor and have not been independently verified. Even so, the site may contain errors, and it describes ground that changes. It is offered as it is, with no warranty that it is complete, current or fit for any particular purpose. If you find something wrong, please write to hello@cairdecorrin.ie and it will be corrected.

Addresses of the form cairdecorrin.ie/s/1 are reserved for interpretation signage that is in development. They are permanent. Once a sign carries one, it will keep resolving to the content for that stop even if the site is rebuilt.
