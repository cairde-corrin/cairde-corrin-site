Strand 2 - New spaces

Two projects in early development.

Both sit inside the action area of about 990 hectares. Neither is agreed yet, and both are described here as proposals rather than plans.

In development

Corrin moorland

Upland habitat on the Corrin side of the action area, outside the Glenabo catchment but part of the same walk and the same landscape.

The proposed route

The phased approach runs from interpretation on existing trails through to a single connected loop.

Phase 1

Glenabo interpretation

Trail signage with codes linking to ecology, biodiversity, flora, fauna, folklore and history, and to guidance on responsible recreation.

Phase 2

Corrin interpretation

The same approach applied at Corrin, reflecting its own ecological, archaeological and landscape character.

Phase 3

Glenabo extension

Roughly 600 metres of new trail to the rear of the existing route, reaching a forest track opposite the road to Waste Recovery Services.

Phase 4

The full loop

Linking Glenabo to Corrin to give a connected route of up to 15 km, joining Fermoy Quays, Barnane, Glenabo Woods, Corrin Woods and the Cork Road.

Any works would be subject to Coillte consent, environmental assessment including screening for Appropriate Assessment where required, and compliance with health and safety standards.
