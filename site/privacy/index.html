Privacy

What this site
collects about you.

Almost nothing. This is a set of static files. It runs no analytics, sets no cookies and loads nothing from another company's servers.

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking

The site sets no cookies. It stores nothing in your browser beyond the ordinary cache. There is no analytics package, no tag manager, no advertising pixel and no social media widget. Nothing on any page reports your visit to a third party.

Every font, image, stylesheet, script, map file and audio file is served from this site. Nothing is loaded from a content delivery network, a font service or an image host, so no other company sees your request simply because you opened a page here.

Links out

Several pages link to other sites: Coillte, dúchas.ie, the National Biodiversity Data Centre, Wikimedia Commons, Invasive Species Ireland, catchments.ie and others. Those are ordinary links. Nothing loads from them until you choose to follow one, and once you do you are on their site under their privacy policy, not this one.

The map, and your position

The map on the trails page has a button that shows where you are. If you press it, your browser will ask your permission and then work out your position on your device. That position is drawn on the map in your browser and is not sent to this site, stored, or shared with anyone. If you never press the button, your position is never requested. If you refuse the permission, the rest of the map works exactly as before.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify's servers process the technical information your browser sends in order to deliver the page: your IP address, the page requested, the time, and your browser and operating system. Cáirde Corrin does not read those logs and does not receive a copy of them. What Netlify does with them is governed by Netlify's own privacy notice and its terms as a processor.

Email

If you write to hello@cairdecorrin.ie, we hold your message and your email address for as long as we need them to deal with what you wrote about, and to keep a record of what was agreed. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not pass your address to anyone else. There is no sign-up form anywhere on this site.

Photographs of people

No photograph on this site shows an identifiable person. Written consent is held for the photographs in the group’s archive that do, so any published here later will carry it.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you may ask what personal data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and complain to the Data Protection Commission. Because the only personal data we hold is email correspondence you started, a request will usually be answered by telling you what is in the inbox and deleting it if you ask. Write to hello@cairdecorrin.ie.

Changes

If the site starts doing something this page does not describe, this page changes first. Anything added later that collects information, such as a sign-up form or an event booking, will be described here before it goes live.
