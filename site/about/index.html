About us

A community group
working to a catchment.

Cáirde Corrin is a voluntary group in Fermoy, Co. Cork. We work on the Glenabo and Corrin woodlands and the waterways that run through them.

Our vision

Glenabo-Corrin is an historical land where all living things can thrive in harmony with nature.

Our mission

We work to protect, restore and celebrate the environment, heritage and recreational use of our Glenabo-Corrin waterways, engaging with our local community, partnering with agencies, and taking action to ensure the rivers and their valleys flourish for future generations.

Who we work with

Landowner

Coillte

Owner and manager of Glenabo and Corrin woods. All works on their land are subject to their consent and assessment procedures.

How to get involved

You do not need to be an ecologist. Most of what needs doing is walking, looking, recording and turning up. Get in touch and we will tell you what is happening next.
